Programs & certifications

Programs & certifications

At CRISOSA we work with internationally recognized programs and certifications to ensure that our services meet the highest standards of quality, safety and sustainability.

IMMEX Program

Allows the temporary importation of goods for industrial or service processes without paying import taxes, to subsequently be exported.

IVA/IEPS Certification

Enables companies to temporarily import goods without paying taxes, receive tax credit, faster VAT refunds and extended permanence of goods.

AAA

CTPAT Certification

A customs security program that offers benefits to companies meeting security standards, streamlining their import processes.

OEA Certification

A program aimed at strengthening the security of the foreign trade logistics chain through internationally recognized minimum security standards, in coordination with the private sector.
